About OFAG

The Office of the Federal Auditor General (OFAG) is responsible for ensuring the transparency and accountability of the country’s executive and its various entities, which provide public services to the citizens. The government and public financial resources, as well as movable assets, must be managed in accordance with the law, regulations, and directives. To ensure proper management, OFAG conducts legal audits to verify that these resources are being used in compliance with established laws, thereby promoting transparency and accountability. OFAG, with more than 80 years of experience in providing audit services, remains an independent and impartial government institution, offering free and autonomous services.

The Office of the Federal Auditor General’s mandate primarily extends to overseeing the audit operations of the federal government, as defined by the Constitution of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia, Article 101, and the existing regulatory framework under the establishment Proclamation No. 982/2008. Furthermore, its work is aligned with the 2011 amendment to the Proclamation (No. 1146/2011), which outlines specific powers and responsibilities to further enhance the effectiveness of audit operations. Based on these authorities, OFAG conducts its work efficiently, ensuring quality outcomes, which are then formally reported to the House of Peoples' Representatives (HOPR) to be made public. In addition, it is responsible for executing other legal mandates assigned by law.

Contribute towards enhancing good governance and effectiveness of the government institution's performance by undertaking independent audit services based on International standards for supreme audit institutions.

The Office of the Federal Auditor General (OFAG) is an institution established under the Constitution and Proclamations of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia (FDRE), mandated to report to the House of Peoples' Representatives. It is a legally autonomous, independent, and impartial entity that operates in accordance with international auditing standards and contemporary best practices, ensuring its work is timely, credible, and relevant.

OFAG undertakes auditing activities with the aim of promoting national and governmental transparency and accountability, ultimately contributing to the development of a governance system that delivers benefits and services to the public. Through its audits, OFAG plays a vital role in uncovering institutional inefficiencies and governance failures, ensuring these findings are made publicly accessible and that the responsible bodies are held accountable.

The audit reports, which are based on thorough findings and supporting evidence, are presented to the legally mandated government bodies, facilitating the creation of appropriate conditions for accountability. These efforts have enabled OFAG to register impactful outcomes. Currently, OFAG is enhancing its performance by integrating modern technologies and updated auditing frameworks, enabling it to deliver on its mandate with greater efficiency.

Audit recommendations carry legal weight and audited entities are expected to implement them according to the timelines and actions specified in their audit responses.

Members of the public can submit audit requests through OFAG's official feedback or complaint channels. Requests are screened and prioritized based on risk, materiality, and strategic priorities.

Annual reports are published on OFAG's official website under the publications section and archived for public access in downloadable formats.

OFAG reports directly to the House of Peoples' Representatives, maintaining operational independence while providing the legislature with audit findings and recommendations.

Auditor General's Message

Federal Auditor General

The Office of the Federal Auditor General (OFAG) is an institution established under the Constitution and Proclamations of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia (FDRE), mandated to report to the House of Peoples' Representatives. It is a legally autonomous, independent, and impartial entity that operates in accordance with international auditing standards and contemporary best practices, ensuring its work is timely, credible, and relevant.

OFAG undertakes auditing activities with the aim of promoting national and governmental transparency and accountability, ultimately contributing to the development of a governance system that delivers benefits and services to the public. Through its audits, OFAG plays a vital role in uncovering institutional inefficiencies and governance failures, ensuring these findings are made publicly accessible and that the responsible bodies are held accountable.

The audit reports, which are based on thorough findings and supporting evidence, are presented to the legally mandated government bodies, facilitating the creation of appropriate conditions for accountability. These efforts have enabled OFAG to register impactful outcomes. Currently, OFAG is enhancing its performance by integrating modern technologies and updated auditing frameworks, enabling it to deliver on its mandate with greater efficiency.

Moreover, the Office continues to disseminate audit findings and accurate, trustworthy information to the public and relevant stakeholders in a timely manner. In turn, it collects feedback from citizens and stakeholders to improve governance practices and foster a more responsive administrative system.

The Office verifies the conformity of organizational practices with legal and regulatory standards, highlights systemic weaknesses, and ensures that corrective measures are taken in a timely manner.

The successful fulfillment of this mandate relies heavily on close collaboration with the House of Peoples' Representatives and other stakeholders. In particular, by submitting well-substantiated reports backed by credible evidence and detailed findings, OFAG supports legal institutions in holding public officials accountable under the rule of law.

To further enhance its effectiveness, OFAG continues to modernize its internal systems, expand its institutional capacity, and leverage cutting-edge technology. Continuous professional development of staff, both locally and internationally, is pursued to ensure a competent and dynamic workforce. These efforts aim to foster a results-oriented and efficient audit environment.

The Office actively engages in strategic partnerships with international audit institutions to ensure alignment with global audit standards and practices. Notably, OFAG maintains productive cooperation with AFROSAI-E (African Organization of English-speaking Supreme Audit Institutions), a partnership that is proving to be mutually beneficial and strategically valuable.

Despite ongoing efforts to build a highly skilled and professional workforce, challenges such as high staff turnover—often linked to compensation and benefits—persist. OFAG is engaging relevant government bodies to propose viable solutions that would help mitigate this issue and retain qualified professionals.

OFAG prepares its annual operational plans based on five-year strategic frameworks, developed in a participatory manner involving all staff. The execution of these plans is regularly monitored and evaluated to ensure impact and relevance. As a result of its structured planning and rigorous implementation, the Office has successfully achieved 100% financial audit coverage and increased performance audit coverage to 26% in recent years.

At present, the Office is implementing its 2020–2025 institutional strategic plan, with annual plans being derived and executed accordingly. Furthermore, in collaboration with the Standing Committee on Government Expenditure Administration and Oversight, OFAG organizes public forums where audit findings are presented and discussed. The feedback and recommendations from these engagements serve as a basis for institutional reforms and improved accountability.

The Office has recorded notable achievements over the past years in the auditing landscape. These successes are the result of addressing operational challenges proactively and engaging in solution-focused dialogue with stakeholders.

This has enabled the Office to continuously strengthen its performance—thanks to the unwavering commitment, integrity, and professionalism of its leadership and staff.

Looking ahead, OFAG remains fully committed to tackling foreseeable governance challenges through strategic preparedness and proactive problem-solving. It is my firm belief that the Office will continue to deliver impactful results and serve as a model of excellence in public sector performance and accountability.

Given the magnitude of the national responsibility placed on OFAG—to safeguard public resources from mismanagement and corruption—every leader and staff member of the institution is entrusted with upholding the highest standards of professionalism, ethical conduct, and integrity in fulfilling their duties.

Thank you.

Meseret Damte

Federal Auditor General
